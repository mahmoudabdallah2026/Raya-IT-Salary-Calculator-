METHODOLOGY & ASSUMPTIONS

Income tax uses Egypt's seven progressive brackets under Income Tax Law No. 91/2005 as amended (0% up to EGP 40,000 → 27.5% above EGP 1,200,000) with a EGP 20,000 annual personal exemption, applied to salary income after deducting employee social-insurance contributions. The high-earner bracket-melting rule is applied: the lower brackets are progressively withdrawn as annual taxable income passes EGP 600k, 700k, 800k, 900k and 1,000k, leaving only the top two brackets above EGP 1,000,000. Social insurance follows Law No. 148/2019 — employee 11%, employer 18.75% (or 21% flat for directors on the commercial register) on the insurable wage, floored at EGP 2,700 and capped at EGP 16,700 per month from 1 January 2026. Comprehensive health insurance, the Martyrs Fund and the exact insurable base can vary by payroll practice, so they are configurable above.
